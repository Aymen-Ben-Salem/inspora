# Inspora

Inspora presents creators' work in archives, creator profiles, and saved collections. This glossary covers public-work eligibility and creator identity.

## Language

**Work**:
A creator's design, logo, or website entry; app icons are a kind of logo.
_Avoid_: Post as the umbrella term for all work.

**Design**:
A work presented in a design category, such as Web, Branding, or Motion. A Web-category design is distinct from a website entry.
_Avoid_: Website when referring only to a Web-category design.

**Published work**:
Work whose publication is enabled and whose publication time has arrived. Publication alone does not establish presentation completeness.
_Avoid_: Publicly eligible work when only publication is known.

**Publicly eligible work**:
Published work that meets the presentation requirements for its kind at the time it is read. Membership in a saved collection or attribution to a creator does not grant public eligibility.
_Avoid_: Published work as shorthand for all eligibility checks having passed.

**Website presentation completeness**:
The presence of a website recording with its poster and video preview, a favicon, and at least one section, with an image and dimensions for every section.
_Avoid_: Recording completeness when the requirement also includes favicon and sections.

**Collection scope**:
The membership restriction used to select work, such as an archive, a creator's work, or a viewer's saved collection. Public eligibility applies within that scope.

**Saved work**:
Work a viewer has bookmarked. The bookmark can remain even when the work is temporarily ineligible for public display.
_Avoid_: Saved post when referring to designs, logos, and websites together.

## Creator identity

**Creator**:
The identity credited for work on Inspora, whether or not an account manages it.
_Avoid_: User, account, or profile as synonyms for creator.

**Creator account**:
An account whose active status permits its user to manage an owned creator and pursue ownership claims.
_Avoid_: Creator when referring to the account or its lifecycle.

**Creator ownership**:
The association granting an account's user management of a creator. Work attribution alone does not establish ownership.
_Avoid_: Authorship or work ownership as synonyms for management rights.

**Work attribution**:
The credit linking a work to a creator, independent of publication and creator ownership.
_Avoid_: Claim when referring to credit alone.

**Creator profile**:
The public-facing name, username, avatar, and links that present a creator.
_Avoid_: Account when referring to this presentation.

**Creator username**:
The creator's current Inspora profile name used in its public address.
_Avoid_: Handle or X username when referring to the Inspora username.

**Creator username alias**:
A reserved Inspora username that resolves to a creator, including its current username and retained former usernames.
_Avoid_: Available username for a retained former username.

**Verified X identity**:
An X account supported by verified provider evidence, identified by its provider account ID and accompanied by its current username and profile URL.
_Avoid_: X profile URL as proof of identity or creator ownership.

**Creator ownership claim**:
A request to associate a user's verified X identity and ownership with an existing creator, subject to review.
_Avoid_: Verified creator or claimed creator when only a pending request exists.

**Claim target creator**:
The existing creator an ownership claim requests to manage and whose identity survives an approved merge.
_Avoid_: Editorial creator as a synonym for every claim target.

**Provisional creator**:
The requester's currently owned creator in the context of a claim, merged into a distinct claim target on approval.
_Avoid_: Temporary creator as a general label for every user-created creator.

**Owner-edited profile field**:
A profile field explicitly edited by the owner, whose value takes precedence over the claim target's value during a merge.
_Avoid_: Edited field when the distinction between owner and admin edits matters.

**Creator record origin**:
The provenance of a creator record, distinct from its current ownership and claim status.
_Avoid_: Unclaimed creator as a synonym for editorial origin.

Hey guys, quick update on Inspora 👋

I’ve finished the MCP build and would like you to test it before I move on to credits and payments.

What I’ve done

  • Reviewed and enriched the data for over 550 posts: corrected or added descriptions, categories, Industry, Style and Color wherever needed.
  • Added hidden descriptions for search: these describe the work in more detail so searches can find relevant designs beyond their titles and categories. They also cover typography styles where useful.
  • Added a Generate button in the admin for uploading posts: it suggests descriptions, classifications and hidden search details when uploading a post. You can review and edit everything before saving. I also improved the upload page to make that process easier.
  • Improved search relevance: worked on filtering out unrelated results, finding similar designs and letting users explore more without repeating the same references. New posts and edits sync automatically.
  • Built two companion skills: one helps find and refine references; the other helps compare them and develop a visual direction. You don’t need to mention the skills by name, just ask naturally.
